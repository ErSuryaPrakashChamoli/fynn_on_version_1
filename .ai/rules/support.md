# Support

## Two hierarchy walks: visibleSubordinateIds() sees, subordinateIds() counts
These are deliberately different questions — do not merge them.

- visibleSubordinateIds() (added 2026-09-10) walks the tree WITHOUT dropping an exited Team Leader / Manager. Marking somebody inactive only sets employees.exit_status; it never reassigns their customers, leads or follow-ups. The old walk skipped an exited level, which took the still-working callers under them out of their Manager's and Cluster Manager's sight and left those live cases visible to nobody but the Admin. Use it for record visibility only: CustomerResource::getEloquentQuery(), FollowUpResource, CustomerJourneyAccessService::hasNormalAccess().
- subordinateIds()/callerIds() leave out exited intermediate levels and stay the walk for target, incentive and target-setting maths (AchievementCalculatorService, MonthlyTargetGate). Since 2026-11-24 the people under an exited level are NOT dropped: they count under the nearest boss still on the rolls on their own record (ReportingTree count_boss_id, user decision Q13 — see targets.md); the exited level itself and its ₹80L Team Leader minimum are left out.

tests/Feature/ExitedEmployeeRecordVisibilityTest.php asserts both halves, including that the two walks disagree (the visibility walk carries the exited Team Leader, the maths walk does not).

Leads/Assigned Leads use HierarchyService::visibleEmployeeIds(), which never filtered on exit status and needs no change. Note it also gives a Manager their DIRECT callers (manager_id), which neither HierarchyHelper walk does — a caller reporting straight to a Manager is visible in Leads but not in Customers.

## Every hierarchy walk comes from ReportingTree direct-boss links
Supersedes the designation-by-designation walks (2026-09-15). ReportingTree::load() reads employees once and links each person to their direct boss: the nearest filled Employee::REPORTING_COLUMNS entry that points at THAT column's designation and sits above their own rank. Wrong-level columns are ignored — live data has callers with a Manager or even a Caller in superviser_id, and exited Manager #67's cluster_id holds a Manager. A caller's copied manager_id/cluster_id are never used to walk.

The two walks still differ: descendantIds(skipExitedLevels: true) for targets/incentives/target duty — following count_boss_id, the nearest active boss on the employee's own record (2026-11-24) — false for visibility (direct boss_id links). The trees are shared per request through App\Support\ReportingSnapshot (2026-11-21, supersedes "never cache the tree"): employeeRows(), load(), ReportingTimeline::between() and the history reads are memoised, and AppServiceProvider clears the memo on ANY executed write naming employees/employee_reporting_history (Eloquent or DB::table) and on every transaction rollback, so a walk after a change in the same request/test still sees it. Measured: one month's timeline is ~100 ms, and the top-performer marquee built it 52 times per dashboard load (2.3 s). Never add another per-call read of those tables or a cache that is not retired the same way; never mutate a row from employeeRows() (clone it first). Scoped binding, keyed by connection (the /demo context switches it). Do not add new where('manager_id'|'cluster_id', ...) walks elsewhere — go through HierarchyHelper/ReportingTree. branchRootId() (Team Continuity pool) never climbs past a Cluster Manager to a Business Head. Covered by tests/Feature/SkipLevelReportingTest.php.

## Period figures walk the tree of each day (ReportingTimeline)
User decision 2026-11-14, superseding "reporting changes restate old months": if A reports to B until 25 Oct and to C from 26 Oct, months before stay with B, October is split, November is C's. ReportingTimeline rebuilds the tree for every day of a period from employee_reporting_history (joining row = line from the start of time; other rows = line from effective_date, the line before an employee's first non-joining row is its old_* columns; exit rows never move anyone; the LATEST line is always today's columns, so untracked edits still apply from the last recorded change). ReportingTree::load($date) = the tree that day; with a date, a level counts as exited only after its exit_date.
- Achievement/OTPs/stage counts/attendance: HierarchyHelper::scopeToBranchDuring() — each row counts for the team its owner was in on its date (disbursal_date, created_at, login_at). Don't add another whereBetween on that column.
- Target: SUPERSEDED 2026-11-24 — see targets.md (TargetLedger). A caller who moved is split by days between the teams they sat in while on the rolls (a team under 10 days gets nothing, the rest share; none reaching 10 → all share by days), so sibling teams add up to their parent again; a Team Leader carries at least ₹80L (no more ₹30L top-up for < 3 callers, no understaffedTeamLeaderIds()). getTargetForPeriod() still decides each month by designationFor() (designation held on the month's last day).
- Headcount (TL caller count + tier, eligible callers/PPP — no longer any target top-up): ReportingTimeline::headcountCallerIds() — every team the caller spent MIN_HEADCOUNT_DAYS (10)+ days in while on the rolls, reporting date to exit date (daysOnTheRolls() via OnRollsWindow), so a mid-month move can count in both, a joiner from the 25th counts nowhere, and a caller who left counts only if they had 10 days that month (user decision 2026-11-16, superseding the 2026-08 rule that exited callers always count). OnRollsWindow is the single home of the reporting-date/exit window; AchievementCalculatorService::activeWindowForMonth() delegates to it.
- Team listings for a month use childrenDuring()/directReporteesDuring(); rows pass $within (getPerformance/getTarget) so they show only the days spent in that team and add up to its total. PPP and incentive stay the person's own.
- Today-only walks (subordinateIds/children/visibleSubordinateIds, ReportingTree::load()) remain for record visibility and today's duties (target gate, escalations, delegations). Daily Commitment period reports still use today's team (not yet split by date).
With no change in the month every figure equals the old whole-month figure. Covered by tests/Feature/ReportingChangeMonthWiseTest.php.
- Designations are dated too (2026-11-16): history rows with change_type 'designation' (ReportingLineService::CHANGE_DESIGNATION, old/new_designation) give each day the designation held then; ReportingTree links a boss only through the column of THAT designation, so without them a Manager promoted in November lost their whole October team (target/achievement 0 — Employee Lookup ME0045). Write them with recordDesignationChange() next to the team re-slot (EditEmployee, EmployeeImporter), same effective date. linesFromHistory() and applyColumns() skip 'designation' rows. Incentive scheme = designation on the month's last day (AchievementCalculatorService::designationFor). Migration 2026_11_16_105540 backfilled older promotions from the "Follows <name>'s designation change." rows. Covered by tests/Feature/DesignationChangeMonthWiseTest.php.
